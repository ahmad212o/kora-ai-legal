Kora AI: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Who uses Kora AI

Kora AI is a private tool used only by its operator to post their own football prediction videos to their own accounts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

2. What data is handled

  • Access tokens for the operator's own accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook), obtained through each platform's official sign-in. They are stored only on the operator's computer, are never shared, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings.
  • Basic profile information of the operator's own TikTok account (e.g. display name), used only to confirm which account videos are posted to.
  • Publishing records (date, platform, video ID, link) kept locally so the same video is never posted twice.

3. What Kora AI does not do

It does not collect data about other people, does not read other users' content, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

4. Public data used to make videos

Match fixtures, results and team statistics come from public football data sources. No personal data of viewers is processed.
