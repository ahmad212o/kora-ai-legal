Kora AI: Terms of Service

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. What Kora AI is

Kora AI (كورة AI) is a private publishing tool used by its operator to create short Arabic football videos that show a statistical prediction for upcoming matches, and to post those videos to the operator's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Use of TikTok

Kora AI uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only to post videos created by the operator to the operator's own TikTok account, and only after the operator has reviewed and approved each video. It does not post on behalf of anyone else, and it does not read, collect or store other TikTok users' content or data.

3. Content

Videos contain statistical predictions for entertainment and discussion only. They are not betting advice. The narration is generated with an AI voice and is labelled as such. Predictions are experimental and are not claimed to be accurate.

4. No warranty

Kora AI is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. The operator is responsible for the content posted.
